# Evaluation and retrospective mode

Production research can use published empirical results as evidence. A retrospective
experiment instead needs a preregistered cutoff and generator/evaluator separation.
The evaluator prepares version-pinned permissible literature and a scrubbed problem brief.
Keep target answers, methods, titles, abstracts, distinctive terms, future revisions and
revealing citation paths outside generation access. Removing numbers alone is insufficient.
Record masked fields, corpus hashes and who had access. Do not send an entire target paper
to the generator to ask it to redact itself. If isolation cannot be enforced, label the
exercise contaminated/unverified and do not report historical discovery performance.

Reference proposals may retain the intended method and evaluation but exclude measured
scores, comparative outcomes and headlines. Those references belong only to evaluation.
This workflow does not reproduce the upstream data construction or its trained reward.
Cutoff filtering controls accessed documents, not knowledge already in model parameters.

Compare three conditions on the same model, brief, fixed corpus and declared caps:
A: a strong single-pass research prompt with identical honesty/evidence constraints;
B: the stages with only same-problem retrieval (cross-problem retrieval ablated);
C: the full skill. Actual counts matter, including retries and failures. A runs in a
clean context with no skill, repository AGENTS.md or generated artifacts. No condition
may read another condition's candidates. Separate real-time coverage experiments.

Report source existence and support separately, decisive near-match identification,
real mechanism differences, constraints, executable/falsifiable experiments, and counts
of valid completions, justified refusals and failures. Record usage and available cost
without inventing unavailable billing. Do not equate citation-set F1 with factual accuracy.
Randomize blind presentation order, repeat when budget permits, and use human review for
important samples; LLM judges are auxiliary. Small pilots are case evidence, not an efficacy
claim. Unexecuted cells are NOT_RUN. Do not launch paid/recursive evaluations implicitly.
